Gender Lens

Reading the budget through a gender marker

Every PSDP project carries a Gender Marker (GEN 1–3), Pakistan's Gender-Responsive Budgeting classification of a project's visible contribution to gender equality and women's empowerment, applied at project level and weighted for portfolio analysis. Click a segment to jump to its definition.

Notable Years

Two years worth flagging

2021‑22 — the only near-even year

50.3% infrastructure

vs. 49.7% non‑infrastructure — the closest to parity across the whole FY2020‑27 window; every other year leans infrastructure.

2025‑26 — the infrastructure peak

71.6% of the budget

went to infrastructure — the widest gap between the two categories in the seven‑year dataset.
